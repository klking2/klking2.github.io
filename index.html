Edward AIOS Drive Sync

這是一個供擁有人在自己的 Mac 上接續工作的私人 AIOS 工具。每部電腦保留自己的資料庫與登入，透過指定的私人 Google Drive 資料夾交換已保存的工作進度、背景、技能、已確認材料及接收收據。

This is a personal workflow tool for its owner to continue work across their own Macs. Each computer keeps its own database and sign-in. Selected saved progress, background, skills, confirmed materials and reception receipts are exchanged through a designated private Google Drive folder.

為何需要 Google 授權

只要求 Google Drive 的 drive.file 權限，存取由工具建立或使用者明確提供給工具的特定檔案，用於交付與回讀核對。不要求讀取整個 Drive、Gmail 或付款資料。

The app requests only the Google Drive drive.file scope to deliver and verify specific files created by, or explicitly made available to, the app. It does not request full Drive, Gmail or payment access.

只公開工具用途、私隱政策及使用說明；不提供工作資料、帳戶登入或同步檔案下載。

This site contains general information only. It provides no access to private work data, sign-in credentials or synchronization files.
